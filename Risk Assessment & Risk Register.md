## 📖 Scenario

You've joined a new cybersecurity team at a commercial bank. The team is conducting a risk assessment of the bank's current operational environment and is building a risk register to help focus on securing the most vulnerable risks.

A risk register is a central record of potential risks to an organization's assets, information systems, and data. Security teams commonly use risk registers when conducting a risk assessment.

For each recorded risk, the process is to:

1. Determine how **likely** the risk is to occur.
2. Determine how **severely** it may impact the bank.
3. Calculate a **priority score**, then compare scores across all risks so the team knows where to focus first.

<!-- ===================== CRITERIA ===================== -->
## 📋 Evaluation Criteria Definitions

| Term | Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| 💰 **Asset** | The asset at risk of being harmed, damaged, or stolen. |
| ⚠️ **Risk(s)** | A potential risk to the organization's information systems and data. |
| 📝 **Description** | A vulnerability that might lead to a security incident. |
| 📈 **Likelihood** | Score from 1 to 3 of the chances of a vulnerability being exploited. |
| 💥 **Severity** | Score from 1 to 3 of the potential damage the threat would cause to the business. |
| 🧮 **Priority** | Likelihood × Severity. |

| Score | Likelihood | Severity |
| :-: | :--- | :--- |
| `1` | Low likelihood | Low severity impact |
| `2` | Moderate likelihood | Moderate severity impact |
| `3` | High likelihood | High severity impact |

<!-- ===================== ANALYSIS ===================== -->
## 🧠 Analysis of the Risks

| Risk | Why It Ranks Where It Does |
| :--- | :--- |
| 🔴 **Financial records leak** | A publicly accessible server of backed up data is easy to find and exploit, and the damage would be high, so it scores the maximum of 9. |
| 🟠 **Compromised user database** | Poorly encrypted customer data across 2,000 individual and 200 commercial accounts would cause high damage, but exploiting it takes more effort. |
| 🟡 **Business email compromise** | With 120 employees handling data, someone being tricked is plausible, but the impact is moderate. |
| 🟢 **Theft** | An unlocked safe would be very damaging, but the bank's area has low crime, which keeps the likelihood low. |
| 🟢 **Supply chain disruption** | Delivery delays from natural disasters are possible in a coastal area, but the impact on the bank is moderate at most. |

> 💡 **Conclusion:** The team should address the publicly accessible backup database first, then the poorly encrypted customer data. These two risks involve sensitive financial data and carry the highest severity, and the first one is also highly likely to be exploited.
